Application behaviour

Closing your TV browser window leaves AussieTV running in the notification area near the clock. Right-click the AussieTV tray icon to reopen it, open Settings, inspect logs or fully exit the application.

Windows Firewall prompts for approval when LAN feed or HDHomeRun discovery permissions change. Allow only on your Private home network. Never forward TCP 45782, TCP 80 or UDP 65001 on your router. Your local-only feed does not require firewall changes.
